Support

Stuck, found a bug, or lost a purchase? Write to chethan.2106@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

How do I play?

Left thumb: drag to move, flick up (or tap) to jump, flick sideways twice to dash, pull down to crouch.

Right thumb: tap to punch, swipe to kick (the direction picks the kick), hold to block. Touch just as a hit lands to parry.

Doodles: when the ink bottle glows, tap it and draw anywhere: ○ shield, ⚡ zigzag lightning, — bridge, | wall, > giant pencil, spiral vortex. There are secret shapes too.

You can replay the training any time from Settings.

My purchase didn't arrive

Open Shop (or Settings) and tap Restore. Permanent purchases (Double Ink, Starter Sketchbook items) come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Gold Nibs and boost crates are credited the moment the App Store confirms payment. If they're missing, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Nearby play can't find my friend

Both devices need Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on and InkFist allowed on the local network: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › InkFist. One player taps Host Nearby, the other Join Nearby, and the host's name appears to join.

Online play / leaderboards

Online matches, Daily Ink leaderboards and achievements use Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center.

My progress

Progress saves on your device and syncs through your iCloud account (Settings › [your name] › iCloud). A new device signed in to the same account picks it up.
